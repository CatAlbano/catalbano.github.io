Cat Albano is from Louisiana and has earned a Masters of Letters in Publishing Studies from the University of Stirling. She has been writing fiction her entire life, and though her style is varied, her novels are often SFF and her short stories embrace realism and a love for her native state. She has a special place in her heart for female characters that are unlikeable or struggle socially. Currently, she is querying Firebelly, a 85,400 word soft sci-fi novel.
